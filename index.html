About Me

My interests are in scattering amplitudes, primarily in the interplay between the moduli spaces of graphs and/or curves with Quantum Field Theory amplitudes. In particular, I have been working on Tropicalised Quantum Field Theory, Surfaceology and related problems on moduli spaces.

Publications

Tropicalised Quantum Field Theory in Momentum space

with Michael Borinsky.

To appear 26xx.xxxx. We discuss Tropicalised Quantum Field Theory in momentum space, deriving the tropical feynman integrals of a scalar QFT from the momentum representation of Feynman Integrals. Using this new perspective, we discuss preserving kinematic dependence in the tropical limit as well as tropicalising fermionic theories. .
